Frequently asked questions

 
What does digital marketing for cladding companies include?

It covers content marketing, PPC advertising, SEO, B2B outreach and lead generation, all built around the services you actually sell, such as cladding remediation, external wall reviews or facade installation.

How is PPC advertising for cladding industry businesses different from general PPC?

Cladding buyers search for very specific services, often at the specification stage. PPC advertising for cladding industry businesses therefore relies on tightly themed keywords, location targeting and a dedicated landing page for each service, rather than broad terms that attract irrelevant clicks.

Can you guarantee first-page Google rankings?

No agency can honestly guarantee a ranking. What we do is build highly targeted, educational content and specific SEO that gives each of your services the best chance of ranking in the top three results for the searches your buyers actually make.

How quickly does lead generation for cladding companies start to deliver?

PPC advertising can generate enquiries within weeks of launch, while content marketing and SEO compound over months. That is why our Essentials package works to a 12-month ROI period.
