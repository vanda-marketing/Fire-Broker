THE BSA & ACCREDITATION EDGE

Compliance is now the selling point

With the Building Safety Act (BSA) placing intense scrutiny on the competency of the "Responsible Person," demand for accredited inspectors and installers has never been higher. We actively match professionals holding recognised credentials — DipFD, BRE, FPA and third-party certifications — with clients who need to prove legislative compliance and maintain a robust "Golden Thread" of safety data.

WORKSTREAM 01

Fire Door Inspection Services

Our introducer service for fire door inspectors isn't just about making you busier — it's about making you busy with the right kind of clients in the right locations. A significant amount of your day is often wasted on travel, administrative bottlenecks and managing bookings. WORKSTREAM 02

Fire Door Maintenance & Upgrade Services

We seamlessly align your operational capacity with client demand — whether you prefer partnering with larger organisations requiring structured, long-term repair frameworks, or smaller businesses needing flexible, reactive support. We segment our maintenance professionals strictly by technical capability and accreditation, matching specialists based on:

  • High-performance specifications: commercial fire door ratings including FD60, FD90 and FD120 systems.
  • Specialist hardware: heavy-duty commercial closers, panic hardware and access control integration.
  • Niche capabilities: heritage and listed building restorations, acoustic and thermal enhancements, and complex structural repairs.
WORKSTREAM 03

Fire Door Installation Services — Supply & Fit

Because a fire door is only as good as its installation, we champion third-party certified installers who can sign off work with absolute legal traceability. We carefully segment our fire door installers into clear areas of expertise, targeting and securing supply-and-fit projects across diverse sectors, including:

  • Commercial & industrial: high-traffic retail, office and manufacturing environments.
  • HMOs & residential blocks: purpose-built student accommodation, build-to-rent sectors and new-build developments.
  • Heritage & listed buildings: specialised, sensitive installations requiring strict aesthetic compliance alongside modern fire safety performance.

Driving compliance & matching through the Building Safety Act

 

The UK regulatory landscape has fundamentally shifted. Under the Building Safety Act, building owners and managers face strict legal penalties if they cannot prove the competency of the contractors working on their life-safety systems.

THE FIRE BROKER ADVANTAGE

We use this to your advantage. We leverage your third-party accreditations — such as BM TRADA (Q-Mark), IFC Certification, FIRAS or SafeContractor — as a premium selling point. By positioning our trade partners as fully compliant, certified experts, we unlock high-value commercial contracts that actively look for verified competency to mitigate their legal risk.
