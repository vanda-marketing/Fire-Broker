Frequently asked questions

 
How does the commission structure work?

Firebroker operates on a fixed-fee membership model. We do not take any commission from your quotes or contracts, so you keep 100% of your earnings.

What accreditations are required to join?

We only partner with professionals who hold recognised UK fire safety accreditations, such as IFE, IFSM or BAFE. All members undergo a rigorous vetting process.

What types of projects can I expect?

Our network spans commercial, heritage and residential fire safety requirements — from universities and hospitals to schools, offices and listed buildings across the UK.

Does Firebroker carry out the works itself?

No. Firebroker is a procurement intermediary and introducer service. We are not a certified fire safety organisation and do not carry out works, provide fire risk assessments, or offer fire safety advice. Our role ends at contractor appointment.
