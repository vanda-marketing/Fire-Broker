THE REGULATORY BACKDROP

Duties that now carry real teeth

Under the Building Safety Act 2022 and Fire Safety Act 2021, Responsible Persons and Accountable Persons must assess and manage external wall risk as part of a documented, ongoing safety case — and maintain a "Golden Thread" of information proving it. Failure to act is no longer just a reputational risk; it carries direct legal and financial exposure.

Specialised workstreams

Two stages of the external wall journey, each matched to the right specialist.
WORKSTREAM 01

External Wall Survey & FRAEW

We introduce you to fire engineers and surveyors who carry out Fire Risk Appraisals of External Walls (FRAEW) in line with PAS 9980:2022, and can issue an EWS1 form where a mortgage lender or freeholder requires one. Assessments typically move through three stages:

  • Desktop documentation review: gathering existing drawings, specifications and construction records to establish what's known about the wall build-up before any physical work begins.
  • Intrusive investigation & material verification: targeted "opening up" works at mapped inspection points to physically sample insulation and confirm combustibility ratings, moving beyond desktop assumptions to forensic, as-built data.
  • Cavity barrier & interface assessment: verifying horizontal and vertical cavity barriers are intact and correctly aligned, and checking fire-stopping at critical junctions such as window jambs, sills and balcony interfaces.
WORKSTREAM 02

Cladding Remediation & Replacement

We match you with certified remediation contractors for the full scope of external wall works, segmented by the type of system involved:

  • ACM & HPL cladding removal: strip-out and replacement of aluminium composite and high-pressure laminate panel systems.
  • Insulation & cavity barrier upgrades: removing combustible insulation such as EPS and reinstating non-combustible, A-rated alternatives with compliant cavity barriers.
  • Balconies & timber elements: remediation of combustible balconies, decking and other external timber features.

Frequently asked questions

 
Do I need an EWS1 form?

Not every building needs one — it depends on height, construction and what your lender or freeholder requires. Our introduced assessors can advise whether a FRAEW to PAS 9980 or a formal EWS1 is the right starting point for your building.

How long does remediation typically take?

Timescales vary enormously by building size, system type and funding route — from a few months for straightforward interim measures to well over a year for full cladding replacement on a high-rise. Your assessor will give you a realistic programme once the survey is complete.

Can Fire Broker help with funding applications?

We don't process applications ourselves, but we connect you with consultants experienced in the Cladding Safety Scheme, Building Safety Fund and developer remediation routes who can guide you through eligibility and the application process.

Does Fire Broker carry out the survey or works itself?

No. Fire Broker is a procurement intermediary and introducer service. We are not a certified fire safety organisation and do not carry out surveys, issue EWS1 forms, or perform remediation works. Our role ends at consultant or contractor appointment.
