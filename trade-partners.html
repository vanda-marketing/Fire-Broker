We don't just tick boxes or rely on paperwork.

Our rigorous, multi-point audit evaluates your business through the eyes of major clients. Frequently asked questions

 
How does the commission structure work?

Firebroker operates on a fixed-fee membership model. We do not take any commission from your quotes or contracts, so you keep 100% of your earnings.

What accreditations are required to join?

We only partner with professionals who hold recognised UK fire safety accreditations, such as IFE, IFSM or BAFE. All members undergo a rigorous vetting process before joining the network.

What types of projects can I expect?

Referrals span commercial, heritage and residential fire safety requirements — from universities and healthcare buildings to schools, offices, retail and listed buildings across the UK.

Is there a minimum contract length?

Core and Essentials packages are billed monthly with no long-term tie-in. Expert packages are scoped individually — get in touch and we'll talk through what fits your business.
